# Phase 04.1 Plan 03: Durable Public Disband Facade Summary

One durable lifecycle backend now reaches created, imported, joined, and loaded groups, with typed public enablement and persist-before-publish disband operations.

## Accomplishments

- Resolved caller-supplied lifecycle storage once by identity, or defaulted to a filtered adapter over the durable group-state backend without an in-memory fallback.
- Threaded lifecycle storage through GroupsManager, GroupFactory, GroupRegistry, MarmotGroup, and GroupSession across create/import/join/load paths.
- Added typed `enableDisbanding()` and `disband()` facade outcomes for success, idempotency, authority/legality rejection, and publication failure.
- Preserved the durable pending request after acknowledgement and pre-ack failure while preventing repeated calls from publishing parallel candidates.
- Verified 50 focused client/session/group/manager tests and the strict library compile with pnpm 10.18.3.

## Decisions Made

- The fallback lifecycle adapter delegates record operations to `groupStateStore` but limits `keys()` and `clear()` to `/disband/` records, preventing lifecycle cleanup from clearing serialized group states.
- Repeated public `disband()` calls return the already-hydrated pending request. Regeneration remains scheduler-owned as required by the plan.
- Publication success is reported as `acknowledged`; canonical terminalization remains a later convergence/settlement concern.
