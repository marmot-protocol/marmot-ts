<objective>
Prove, in one test file, that the three D-04 invalid inputs are refused with the identical structured violation
(reason `account-identity-proof` plus `proofReason`, and `leafIndex` where the refusal is post-apply) on every
legality seam: send, inbound ingest, fork-recovery/pool replay, and tree-fed convergence. Each seam keeps its own
fixed disposition.

Purpose: this is the GRP-02 acceptance gate and the phase's primary defense against the mdk#707 seam-asymmetry
defect class. "Rejected somewhere" is not enough (CONTEXT specifics). Every seam must report the same verdict.
Output: `src/engine/__tests__/account-identity-proof-seams.test.ts`, plus a shared engine-seam fixture helper
extracted (copied) from the existing seam test files so this matrix does not re-derive them.
</objective>

Four `it` blocks:
- **send:** a `MarmotGroupEngine` constructed on `forgedEpoch1` runs `send({ kind: "selfUpdate" })`. It rejects with `name: "CommitLegalityError"`; `project(error.violation)` equals `expected`; the engine epoch stays 1.
- **inbound:** the forged member's raw `createCommit({ state: forgedEpoch1, wireAsPublicMessage: true, ratchetTreeExtension: true, extraProposals: [] })`, wrapped and ingested by an admin1 engine at `adminEpoch1`, yields one `rejected` result with `project(result)` equal to `expected`; the epoch stays 1.
- **replay:** the admin1 engine first sends and confirms its own empty commit. Its re-signed leaf is valid, so it must be accepted, which proves unchanged forged leaves are trusted per D-01. It then ingests the forged self-update envelope. Assert no `processed`, the tip unchanged, and no history edge with that commit digest. The verdict comes from `resolveCandidateParent` on `snapshot(adminEpoch1)` with the forged commit and `adminCallbackFor(...)`.
- **tree-fed:** the admin1 engine sends and confirms its own commit (`ownTag`). The two-deep sibling branch is `sib1` = the forged member's self-update from `forgedEpoch1` and `sib2` = admin2's empty commit, authored after admin2 raw-processes `sib1` with an accept-all callback. Replay both through `buildAdmin1PerspectiveChain(ctx, snapshot(adminEpoch1), [sib1, sib2])`, `recordEdge` both, and assert 2 tips. `reconvergeFromHistory()` keeps `ownTag`, and the lifecycle is `"Stable"`. The verdict comes from `resolveCandidateParent` on `snapshot(adminEpoch1)` with `sib1`.

This proves the abandonment in the tree-fed rows is caused by the proof gate and not by selection or replay mismatch.
