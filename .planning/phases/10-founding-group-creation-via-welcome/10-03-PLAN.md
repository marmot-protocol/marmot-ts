Pinned design decisions for this plan (Claude's Discretion, now fixed — do not re-open):
- `createSimpleGroup` and `createGroup` in `src/core/group.ts` gain **no** invitees parameter. Invitee admission
  happens entirely at the factory/engine layer on the epoch-0 `ClientState` those functions already return
  (PATTERNS `src/core/group.ts` note). `src/core/group.ts` is not modified by this phase.
- `GroupFactory` owns a short-lived founding `MarmotGroupEngine` and constructs the `MarmotGroup` **after**
  confirmation, passing the same `RetainedHistoryStore` and `GroupHistoryTree` instances into it. This is the
  only way to satisfy both D-05 (GroupSession untouched, its `#engine` is private) and D-01 (inherit the CR-09
  recording), and it reuses the shape `GroupRegistry.build` already uses on load.
- `retryWelcome(pubkey)` resolves relays exactly as the initial fanout does (recipient NIP-65 inbox, with the
  group's relay list as `deliver`'s existing fallback) and takes **no** epoch guard: RESEARCH Priority Finding
  #1 establishes that a late Welcome is safe because `GroupsManager.#connectGroup`'s backfill filter has no
  `since` bound, provided the group has relays.
